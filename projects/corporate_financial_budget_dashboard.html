Automated Pipeline Specifications
1. Deduplication Strips trailing whitespace and eliminates void/duplicate journals.
2. Date Normalizer Parses 4 dirty date dialects into standard ISO 8601 (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Fuzzy Entity Map Consolidates supplier aliases into unified master corporate entities.
4. Number Sanitizer Strips currency strings, trailing USD tokens, and parses debit parentheses.
5. COA Mapping Maps raw ERP codes into standard P&L driver line items.
