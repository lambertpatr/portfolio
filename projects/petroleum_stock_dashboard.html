Data Science Anomaly Architecture & Production SOP

Mathematical equations, algorithms, and National Petroleum regulatory enforcement standard operating procedures

1. Modified Z-Score with Median Absolute Deviation (MAD)

To protect against extreme fraud outliers skewing standard deviation, the engine computes Modified Z-Scores for road transit shrinkage:

MAD = Median( | X_i - Median(X) | )
M_i = 0.6745 * ( X_i - Median(X) ) / MAD
Threshold: | M_i | > 3.5 flags statistically anomalous fuel loss.
2. Retail Daily Wet-Stock Mass Balance Reconciliation

Evaluates physical underground tank closing dip vs theoretical calculated inventory:

Book_Closing = Opening_Dip + Decanted_Deliveries - Metered_Pump_Sales
Variance = Closing_Physical_Dip - Book_Closing
If Variance < -0.5% of Sales and persistent across 3 consecutive days → UST Tank Leakage Alert.
3. ASTM Table 54B Temperature & Density Volume Correction Factor (VCF)

Compensates for thermal expansion between ambient coastal loading temperatures (28°C - 34°C) and inland higher altitude destinations (18°C - 24°C):

GSV (Gross Standard Volume @ 15°C) = GOV * VCF
VCF = 1.0 - ( T_ambient - 15.0 ) * α_thermal
α = 0.0012 (PMS), 0.00085 (AGO), 0.00095 (IK)
